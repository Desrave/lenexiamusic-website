Wie is LENEXIA?

Netherlands & Spain based LENEXIA fuses old- and new-school influences with latin house and afro house, layered with fierce tribal beats into a powerful, tropical club sound — standing for a new day of creative freedom and radical energy.

Known for her high-energy, genre-blending sets, Desiree delivers an electrifying audiovisual experience that moves any crowd.
