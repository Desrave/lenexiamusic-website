The Sound

Netherlands & Spain based LENEXIA fuses old- and new-school influences with Latin House and Afro House, layered with fierce tribal beats into a powerful, tropical club sound — standing for a new day of creative freedom, radical energy and unstoppable dancefloor movement.

Known for her high-energy, genre-blending sets, LENEXIA delivers an electrifying audiovisual experience that transcends borders and moves any crowd.
